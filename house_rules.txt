# Kopi & Cake · claims we never make in marketing (made up for this exercise)

1. No "best", "No. 1" or "cheapest" claims, in Singapore or anywhere, unless an independent award says so.
2. No health claims about any food or drink.
3. No prices that are not on the current menu.
4. No quoting a customer by name; quote review text only, with its review ID.
